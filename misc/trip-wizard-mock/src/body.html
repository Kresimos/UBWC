The rigging wizard

Two handsets, in flow order. Confirm the kit comes first, straight off the approve/decline deck: it shows what came through packed and lets the kit sec tick on any extra sail or board the club owns. The rigging wizard then works out the masts, extensions and booms, re-running the whole search on every touch, so watch it move: press the stepper, flip the preset, pack a mix, and the numbers roll and the two lists reshuffle.

The wizard is live: the real solver (web/js/rig/pack.mjs) runs in the page against the fixture rack, so nothing there is a drawn number. The confirm screen's board list is a mockup-local stand-in until the Boards sheet is modelled.

Confirm the kit: seeded, then topped up

The two tiles are what the deck delivered, in words: sails packed, boards packed. They count live as rows toggle.

Below them the whole club inventory, sails by size then boards by volume. Packed rows are blue; the rest are plain and one tap adds them. So a trip that only requested 4 sails can leave with 10 without anyone re-opening the picker. Which sizes start packed, and how tight the rows sit, are knobs in src/design.css (--cf-start-sails, --cf-start-boards, --cf-row-pad).

Denser than the wizard's list

No checkbox: size and name only, the row itself carrying the blue. Rows are ~31px against the wizard tester's ~36px, because this list is the full inventory and the wizard's is only the trip.

Continue is where the list passes to the wizard. In the app the wizard then derives one mast, extension and boom per sail and (follow-on) one fin and base per board.

Two controls, the rest is the answer

Rigs at once is the stepper and the headline in one: the busiest moment the trip has to cover. How full to pack is a single dial over the solver's spare-kit and per-piece weights; the raw weights and the score never show.

Everything below the dial is outcome: pieces on the trailer, spares beyond the one set the target needs, and how well each rig is brand-matched.

The hero tint

Green while the trailer can stand up the number asked for, amber the moment the club's kit can't reach it. That crossfade is the whole readout: no sentence spelling out the algorithm underneath it.

The combination highlights

No umbrella heading, no lead paragraph: the screen drops from the pack dial straight into the mixes. Currently incompatible (amber) is the small size-mixes the current trailer can't rig all at once, found automatically, each one a pale highlight in the same green and amber the hero uses. Tap Pack and the solver repacks to protect that mix; the row turns green under Requested.

Test a combination checks any set you tick, a dense list of sails so two of one size stay apart. The rows settle green or amber, and a failure names the one piece that would fix it.

Motion

Headline numbers count to their new value (~0.4s); the results dip while the search runs; highlight rows animate in and out; the hero tint crossfades. All of it is off under Reduce Motion. Timings live in src/design.css.

Where this goes

The wizard replaces the static report at renderWizard() in web/js/screens/trip.js; the confirm screen becomes the stage before it, seeded from trip_items and /api/kit. src/wizard.css and src/confirm.css are both written to drop into web/css/screens.css unchanged.
