The Trips tab, screen by screen

A trip is a named list, not a place. Packing moves nothing: an item's location stays its storage rack for the whole trip, so unpacking is just closing the list. Everything below follows from that.

These are not redrawings. The frames on this page load web/css/tokens.css and web/css/screens.css verbatim and render through a port of the real itemTile(), so the parts of the tile that are not changing are the shipped design itself. Only the four new decorations — voter tags, the check disc, the fault bubble and the grey out chip — are proposals, and each one is on a slider in the tuner.

The six stages

A trip moves in one direction, and every screen below belongs to exactly one stage. What a member may do, and what the kit list even is, changes at each step.

Open questions

Three calls this mockup makes that are worth disagreeing with.

The thumbnail survives the checklist

A checkable row carries the circle and the picture. A trailer is packed by looking at kit, so losing the thumbnail to a checkbox costs more than the roughly 30px it saves.

What that spends is the row's last spare width, which is why the fault is a bubble on the shot in every trip list now, not only the ones with voter tags on them. The pair is tightened by 4px against the row's own gap to buy some of it back.

Where do declines go?

Into their own greyed-out section at the very bottom of the active trip, and nowhere else. That is the screen somebody is on when they are stood at the trailer looking for the board they asked for, so it is where the answer needs to be.

Not on the packing list, which is a job to work through, and not on a finished trip, which is a record of what went. It is deliberately never deleted: someone who asked deserves the answer, not a vanished request.

Are stars worth their slot while packing?

During packing and unpacking the star row is replaced by where the piece lives, because the only question at that moment is which rack it came off. Requesting and active keep the stars, because that is when a member is choosing.

It is the same slot at the same size, so no row changes height as a trip moves between stages. The type rail goes the same way: once a list is grouped under Boards and Sails headings, a rail of type chips above it filters by the thing the list already says.
